### Star Wars Battlefront II (2005) official mod tools assets - LucasArts / Pandemic Studios
Vehicle, aircraft and infantry rifle models (DC-15A, DC-15x, E-11 and others) converted from the BF2_ModTools .msh files
(msh_to_glb.py, unit_models.py, weapon_models.py).
Animations from the mod tools' Animations folder: AT-ST / AT-AT walk cycles (also driving the AT-XT and Lance walker) and the
soldier rifle set (SoldierAnimationBank/human_0) retargeted onto the clone troopers (msh_anim_to_glb.py, infantry_bf2_anims.py).
