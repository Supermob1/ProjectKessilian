## Political control
**Canon**
- The overburdened Old Republic overlooked crimes committed on the distant worlds, Kalee being one of them, and unintentionally caused injustice. ([Kalee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee))
- Then, Grievous launched a series of devastating campaigns on the desolate planet Huk, homeworld of the Yam'rii, and eventually, their enemies unexpectedly sought the Galactic Republic for assistance. ([Kalee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee))
- Then, the Republic and the Jedi Order intervened in the disastrous conflict, supporting the Yam'rii against the invader. ([Kalee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee))
**Legends**
- When Grievous awoke, San Hill made him an offer: He would be rebuilt as a cyborg and become the leader of the Separatist droid army. (clone-wars; [Kalee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends))
- They were quickly recognized as proud, honorable and deadly warriors by both the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. ([Kalee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends))
- Kalee was discovered during the Old Republic era. ([Kalee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends))

## Battles and events
**Legends**
- Most Kaleesh were opposed to the xenophobic Sith Empire, but the species fought on both sides during the conflict, as well as for Darth Malgus' New Empire, or served as bodyguards for the Hutt Cartel. ([Kalee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends))
- The Yam'rii, a technologically superior species of colonizers, invaded Kalee for its only resource - slaves. ([Kalee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends))

## Settlements
**Legends**
- Kaleela was the capital city of the Outer Rim Territories planet Kalee, homeworld of the Kaleesh species. ([Kaleela](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleela))

## Landmarks and facilities
**Canon**
- The warru'zan, the bloodhalls, was a location on the planet Kalee. At some point, the Kaleesh elders had indoctrinated Sidon, later known as the Delphidian pirate Sidon Ithano. ([Warru'zan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Warru'zan))
**Legends**
- Abesmi was an "island-monolith" located in the Jenuwaa Sea on Kalee. It was believed to be the point from which the native gods ascended to heaven. ([Abesmi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abesmi))
- The Ausez Steppes were a region on a planet Kalee. Shrupak temple was located near them. ([Ausez Steppes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ausez_Steppes))
- The Jenuwaa Sea was a small body of water located on Kalee, which was the site of a battle between the Kaleesh and Yam'rii during the Huk War. When Ronderu lij Kummar was killed battling the Yam'ri... ([Jenuwaa Sea](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jenuwaa_Sea))
