## Political control
**Canon**
- Despite the agreement, when the Jedi Order was destroyed at the end of the Clone Wars, the Dagoyan Order acknowledged the Galactic Empire's sovereignty in order to avoid scrutiny by the Empire. (clone-wars; [Bardotta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardotta))
- With the government of Bardotta fully under the control of the Dagoyan Masters, the Frangawl Cult was banned, with practitioners dying out and disappearing through attrition. ([Bardotta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardotta))
- With the theocratic monarchy of the Dagoyan Order fully established, Bardotta reached out into the greater galaxy, avoiding the Galactic Republic and the politics of the civilized galaxy. ([Bardotta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardotta))
**Legends**
- Bardotta was a planet located in the Colonies' Bardotta system that was ruled by the much beloved Queen Julia during the Clone Wars. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Bardotta/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardotta/Legends))
