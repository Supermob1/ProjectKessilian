## Biomes, flora and fauna
**Canon**
- Its surface was dominated by arbozoic trees, found close to settlements with their roots hanging over the edges of the mesas. ([Aleen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aleen))
- Dead arbozoic trees were at one point in the planet's history hollowed out to create the "Aleen colossi," which remain important in Aleena culture. ([Aleen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aleen))
- Formed by the growth of the arbozoic trees, the caverns were built-up of tunnels carved by water, lava, and tectonic shearing. ([Aleen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aleen))
**Legends**
- Maintaining a unique climate and an atmosphere dangerous to the surface-dwelling Aleena, the Underworld was inhabited by the tree-like Kindalo and the mystical being, Orphne. ([Aleen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aleen/Legends))
- Formed by the growth of the unique arbozoic trees, the caverns were also comprised of tunnels carved by water, lava, and tectonic sheering. ([Aleen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aleen/Legends))
