## Stated geographical relationships
- The Dashta Mountains were a mountain range on Ord Cestus, 180 kilometers west of the Zantay Hills and 75 kilometers northwest of Clandes. They were named after Kilaphor Dashta, the first person to map them around 422 BBY. [L] ([Dashta Mountains])
- Yal-Noy was a mountain on Ord Cestus that was located to the north of the Zantay Hills. A river fed by the mountain's snowmelt ran through the hills. [L] ([Yal-Noy])
- The Zantay Hills were a stretch of hills on Ord Cestus. They were east of the Kibo Geothermal Station and Kibo Lake, 180 kilometers east of the Dashta Mountains, and to the south of Yal-Noy. [L] ([Zantay Hills])
- Cestus Penitentiary was a prison built on Ord Cestus by the Galactic Republic in 321 BBY, on land offered by the X'Ting near and under Kibo Lake. When a dozen Cybot Galactica officers were sent there for twenty years following a... [L] ([Cestus Penitentiary])
- Kibo Lake was a lake on Ord Cestus. It was near the Kibo Geothermal Station and west of the Zantay Hills. [L] ([Kibo Lake])
- The Kibo Plateau was an area of the planet of Ord Cestus in the Outer Rim Territories. It was north of the settlement Barrens and south of ChikatLik, and the plateau contained Kibo Lake. [L] ([Kibo Plateau])
