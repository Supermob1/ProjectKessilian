## Political control
**Canon**
- In the first year of the Galactic Empire, Sluis Van was one of the former Separatist Worlds to be integrated into the Galactic Empire on Separatist Reintegration Day. (clone-wars; [Sluis Van](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sluis_Van))
- Count Dooku later turned both Sluis Van and Yag'Dhul over to the side of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. (clone-wars; [Sluis Van](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sluis_Van))
- During the Clone Wars, Sluis Van was represented in the Separatist Senate by Aguth Omak. (clone-wars; [Sluis Van](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sluis_Van))
**Legends**
- During the Clone Wars, Sluis Van was located in Separatist space and the Sluis Van shipyards produced starships for the Confederate forces. (clone-wars; [Sluis Van/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sluis_Van/Legends))
- As well as many other Confederate worlds, it was besieged by Republic forces during the Outer Rim Sieges. (clone-wars; [Sluis Van/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sluis_Van/Legends))
- At the end of the Clone Wars, the Sluis Van system had become a Separatist stronghold. (clone-wars; [Sluis Van/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sluis_Van/Legends))
