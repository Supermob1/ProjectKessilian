**Legends**
- Commenor native Jan Dodonna became one of the first Star Destroyer captains, while Ahalas Svindren became a Major in the Grand Army of the Republic and a prominent Republic propagandist. ([Commenor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commenor/Legends))

## Battles and events
**Canon**
- In the aftermath of the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY, the Empire targeted Commenor as part of Operation: Cinder, attempting to destroy the world after the death of the Emperor. ([Commenor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commenor))
- Thirty years after the Battle of Endor, Commenor was a member world in the New Republic, where it was represented in the Galactic Senate by Thanlis Depallo. ([Commenor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commenor))

- By this time, the New Republic still used the Folor Base, especially during the Core Campaign. ([Commenor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commenor/Legends))
- The facility was attacked by New Republic forces soon after the Thrawn campaign. ([Commenor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commenor/Legends))
- Between 3959 BBY and 3956 BBY, during the Jedi Civil War, a battle was fought here against the Sith, which the Republic won. ([Commenor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commenor/Legends))
