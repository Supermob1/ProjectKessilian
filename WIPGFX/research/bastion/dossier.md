## Political control
**Canon**
- During the Clone Wars, Bastion fell within Separatist territory during the Outer Rim Sieges. (clone-wars; [Bastion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastion))
- Sometime after the Galactic Civil War's end with the collapse of the Galactic Empire by the New Republic, the successor to the Imperial state began to formulate within the Outer Rim. ([Bastion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastion))
**Legends**
- Unlike most of the worlds in the galaxy, the Imperial occupation of Bastion had lasted for the duration of all 157 years of the Galactic Empire's existence, remaining loyal until the end. ([Bastion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastion/Legends))
- In 137 ABY, Emperor Roan Fel reclaimed the planet as a base of operations against the usurper to the Imperial throne, Darth Krayt. ([Bastion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastion/Legends))
- During the Old Republic era, the planet had originally been settled by Humans who opposed alien membership in the Galactic Republic. ([Bastion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastion/Legends))
