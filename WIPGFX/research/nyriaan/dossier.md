## Political control
**Canon**
- Nyriaan was a planet where the human Jenkes managed a gladiator arena during the early days of the Galactic Empire's rule. ([Nyriaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan))
- Nine years after operating the arena, Jenkes, now an Imperial officer in the Imperial Security Bureau, had two gladiators from Nyriaan transported via shuttle to the planet Lothal. ([Nyriaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan))
**Legends**
- Without the restrictions that had been placed on them by the Republic, the CSA and the Mining Guild both expanded their operations and began exploiting native land. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
- An inter-galactic conflict known as the Clone Wars broke out in 22 BBY, between the Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
- The natives resisted violently, and were assisted by the remnants of the Republic forces. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))

## Battles and events
**Legends**
- The Confederacy captured Nyriaan during the First Battle of Nyriaan and the Republic's holdings on the world were annexed and split between the CSA and the Mining Guild. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
- In 19 BBY, a Republic strike force was dispatched to the Luire system and the CIS was defeated in the Second Battle of Nyriaan and driven from the system. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
- However, shortly afterwards, Supreme Chancellor Palpatine, the leader of the Republic, initiated Order 66 and all of the Jedi in the system were killed. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))

## Biomes, flora and fauna
**Legends**
- The planet surface consisted of plains and low hills, with some valleys, where boggy conditions had formed. (low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
- Nyriaan also had a vast number of different plant species, including many different species of fungi, although only 5% of the planet's plant species had been classified by scientists. (low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
- Another plant, a small fungal weed, called the mire nettle, could be processed to create Nyriaan spice, a type of powerful narcotic. (low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))

## Geography
**Legends**
- In the northern hemisphere, there were many shallow, rocky valleys, which were where most of the planet's mining operations took place. (low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
- While Force-users could use the Force when on the planet surface, they couldn't while traveling though the planetary atmosphere, due to an unidentified interference. (low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
- It was a habitable world, with an atmosphere that was made up mainly of nitrogen and oxygen, and a mean temperature of 40 °C. (low confidence; [Nyriaan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaan/Legends))
