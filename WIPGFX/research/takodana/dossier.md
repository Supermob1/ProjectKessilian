## Political control
**Canon**
- During the High Republic Era, Takodana hosted a Jedi Temple that was manned by the Kyuzo Jedi Sav Malagán. ([Takodana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Takodana))
- The lower levels of the castle dated to the occupation of Takodana by the Jedi. ([Takodana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Takodana))
- Takodana fell under the control of Pirate Queen Maz Kanata via a plan that involved her conning the Geonosian Arkik Von out of Takodana, with the entire planet having been a score that Von was after. ([Takodana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Takodana))

## Settlements
**Canon**
- Andui was a city on the Mid Rim planet of Takodana. Using a speeder bike, it took a solid day to get there from the castle of Maz Kanata. ([Andui](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andui))
- The world was connected to the Ring of Kafrene mining colony by the Biox Detour hyperspace route. ([Takodana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Takodana))
