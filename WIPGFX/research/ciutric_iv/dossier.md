## Political control
**Legends**
- Ciutric IV was a member of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Ciutric IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ciutric_IV))
- After the Yuuzhan Vong's defeat, Ciutric IV was situated in Galactic Alliance space, and the world was a member state of Roan Fel's Empire-in-exile as of 137 ABY. ([Ciutric IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ciutric_IV))
- It was controlled by Imperial Governor Brothic, but was later controlled by Prince-Admiral Delak Krennel. (low confidence; [Ciutric IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ciutric_IV))

## Settlements
**Legends**
