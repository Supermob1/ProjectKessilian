## Biomes, flora and fauna
**Legends**
- Corsin was a beautiful world, the surface of which featured landmasses and oceans, with its tropical regions having a warm climate and dry land being home to plants. ([Corsin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corsin/Legends))
- The planet's temperate and tropical waters were home to the aquatic mammal selligore, which was a more-than-twenty-meters-long creature that fed on small fish, plankton, and oxygenating plants. ([Corsin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corsin/Legends))
- It involved the Tinn'lyi leaving ashore abundant amounts of plants for the creatures. ([Corsin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corsin/Legends))
