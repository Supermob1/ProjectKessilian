## Stated geographical relationships
- Lake Sui was a lake located on the Forest moon of Endor. The lake was surrounded by forest and was home to multiple villages that were built on stilts and inhabited by the moon's native Ewok species. [C] ([Lake Sui])
- The Death Star's shield generator was an Imperial Military installation on Endor during the Galactic Civil War. The facility housed the generator that powered the SLD-26 planetary shield generator protecting the DS-2 Death Star... [C] ([Death Star's shield generator])
- Mount Krana was a mountain on Endor, located beyond the Salma Sands. A Gorax whom the Ewok shaman Makrit called "the Great Devourer" lived there. [C] ([Mount Krana])
- The Empire also established other facilities beyond the shield generator complex, including Platform 4, another bunker, and Research Station 9, which was established by the Tarkin Initiative. [C] ([Endor])
- The Happy Grove was a section of forest on the Forest Moon of Endor. Bright Tree Village, home to Chirpa's tribe of Ewoks, was located in the Happy Grove. [L] ([Happy Grove/Legends])
