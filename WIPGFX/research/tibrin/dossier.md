## Political control
**Canon**
- During the High Republic Era, Captain Hedda Casset and the crew of the Legacy Run were tasked with taking a saberfish, which had been taken from the storm seas of Tibrin, to an Abregado-rae countess. ([Tibrin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tibrin))
**Legends**
- Sora Bulq then was made temporary Confederate governor of Tibrin. (clone-wars; [Tibrin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tibrin/Legends))
- During the Clone Wars planet was ruled by the murderous dictator Suribran Tu, who initially sided with the Galactic Republic. (clone-wars; [Tibrin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tibrin/Legends))
- After Count Dooku conquered the world in 22 BBY, Tu offered to work with the Confederacy of Independent Systems, but was instead executed by Dooku to gain the sympathy and support of the Ishi Tib. (clone-wars; [Tibrin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tibrin/Legends))
