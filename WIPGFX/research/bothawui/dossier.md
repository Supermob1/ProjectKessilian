## Biomes, flora and fauna
**Legends**
- Bothawui's mountains and ridges were separated by deep, wide valleys that were inhabited by dangerous wildlife. ([Bothawui/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothawui/Legends))
- Due to a slight wobble in its axial plain, Bothawui experienced numerous small ice ages, which expanded and retracted across the planet's higher latitudes in seventy-year cycles. ([Bothawui/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothawui/Legends))
- Snow covered mountain tops on Bothawui, and as a result of cyclical ice ages, large areas of land on the world would be covered by ice sheets for decades, transforming savannas into rocky wastelands. ([Bothawui/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothawui/Legends))

## Geography
**Legends**
- The planet was 9,000 kilometers in diameter and possessed a breathable atmosphere, standard gravity, a temperate climate, and a moderate hydrosphere. ([Bothawui/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothawui/Legends))
- Due to the presence of mountainous terrain, large tracts of wilderness on Bothawui remained undeveloped and devoid of settlements. ([Bothawui/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothawui/Legends))
- A terrestrial world orbited by three moons, Bothawui had a day that lasted 27 standard hours and a year that consisted of 351 local days. ([Bothawui/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothawui/Legends))
