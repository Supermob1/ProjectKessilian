## Political control
**Canon**
- An exporter of ores, the once prosperous world eventually fell into obscurity after outside forces like the Galactic Empire engaged in strip mining operations. ([Axxila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axxila))
- Axxila was the homeworld of Imperial Admiral Firmus Piett. ([Axxila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axxila))
**Legends**
- When the Hegemony, along with Axxila, joined the Separatists, many Axxilan, known as the Free Axxilans, fled the planet and relocated to the planet Halmad. (clone-wars; [Axxila/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axxila/Legends))
- It was the homeworld of Imperial Admiral Firmus Piett, the last commanding officer of Darth Vader's Death Squadron. ([Axxila/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axxila/Legends))
- Following the end of the Clone Wars, many Separatist forces retreated to the neighboring Ciutric sector. ([Axxila/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axxila/Legends))

## Battles and events
**Legends**
- Several months after the Battle of Endor, Ysanne Isard sent Admiral Delak Krennel to attack the planet in order to prevent Sate Pestage from giving Coruscant to the New Republic. ([Axxila/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axxila/Legends))
