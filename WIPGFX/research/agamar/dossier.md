## Political control
**Canon**
- In 20 BBY, the third year of the Clone Wars, the planet was under the control of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. (clone-wars; [Agamar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar))
- Because of Kalani's override of the droid shutdown signal, an isolated Separatist holdout remained on the planet until well into the era of the Galactic Empire. (clone-wars; [Agamar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar))
- During the Clone Wars, Separatist General Kalani was ordered by Count Dooku to retreat from Onderon to Agamar following the Separatist defeat on Onderon. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Agamar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar))
**Legends**
- Once Separatist forces withdrew from the Thurra system, the Grand Army of the Republic prepared to move back into the Mirgoshir system, in an attempt to secure Agamar. (clone-wars; [Agamar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar/Legends))
- This disaster prompted Kuat Drive Yards to team up with Mekuun to design the Unstable Terrain Artillery Transport, which the Republic used successfully on worlds including Mygeeto and Cato Neimoidia. (clone-wars; [Agamar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar/Legends))
- Later in 22 BBY, war broke out between the Republic and the Separatists. (clone-wars; [Agamar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar/Legends))

## Geography
**Canon**
- Agamar had a surface covered in rocky terrain, with a cold climate. ([Agamar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar))
- It had an atmosphere breathable to humans and Lasats. ([Agamar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar))
**Legends**
- A terrestrial world, Agamar's surface featured several landmasses separated by oceans, including the Bil Da'Gari and the Crescent Sea. ([Agamar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar/Legends))
- Due to the Center's close proximity to the sea, the ocean had a clear influence on the architecture. ([Agamar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar/Legends))
- The climate was warm and dry, though the planet had large expanses of arable land. ([Agamar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agamar/Legends))
