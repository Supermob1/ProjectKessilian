## Geography
**Canon**
- Its environment was so harsh that their organs were sealed away from the atmosphere and they could survive for a short while in vacuum. ([Yag'Dhul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yag'Dhul))
**Legends**
- As a result, Yag'Dhul's oceans and even its atmosphere flowed from one side of the planet to the other in patterns predictable only through the application of advanced mathematics. ([Yag'Dhul/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yag'Dhul/Legends))
- The planet possessed three large moons that orbited one another around a common center of gravity. ([Yag'Dhul/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yag'Dhul/Legends))
- At the same time, the moons and Yag'Dhul itself orbited a second shared center of gravity, as their combined mass amounted to roughly 70 percent of the planet's mass. ([Yag'Dhul/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yag'Dhul/Legends))
