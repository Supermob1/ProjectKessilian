## Political control
**Canon**
- During the Clone Wars, the husband of Confederacy of Independent Systems Senator Mina Bonteri went there to set up a base for the Separatists, but was killed when Republic clone troopers attacked. (clone-wars; [Aargonar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aargonar))
- The planet was also the homeworld of the human Imperial stormtrooper TK-14057. ([Aargonar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aargonar))
**Legends**
- The world was located in space contested by the Galactic Republic and Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. (clone-wars; [Aargonar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aargonar/Legends))
- During the Jedi Civil War, Aargonar was located within space controlled by Darth Revan's Sith Empire. ([Aargonar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aargonar/Legends))
- During the Galactic Civil War, Aargonar's third moon acted as a Rebel Alliance base, but the Rebels were routed by the Galactic Empire around 0 ABY. ([Aargonar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aargonar/Legends))
