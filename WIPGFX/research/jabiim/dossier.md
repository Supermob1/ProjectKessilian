## Geography
**Legends**
- Jabiim possessed an ionized atmosphere that was well-suited for oxygen-breathing species, but made high-altitude repulsorlift vehicles impossible to operate. ([Jabiim/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jabiim/Legends))
- The planet had a temperate, but inhospitable climate that continually experienced storms that could interfere with sensor devices. ([Jabiim/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jabiim/Legends))
- The surface of Jabiim was continually deluged by torrential rains and constant drizzle, and its muddy surfaces were unstable and constantly shifted from the weight of the rains. ([Jabiim/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jabiim/Legends))
