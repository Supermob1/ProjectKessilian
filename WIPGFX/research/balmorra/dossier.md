## Political control
**Canon**
- During the Clone Wars between the Republic and the Separatist Alliance, which began in 22 BBY, Balmorra left the Republic and joined the Separatists, which installed droid factories on the planet. (clone-wars; [Balmorra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balmorra))
- However, the Separatists were ultimately defeated in 19 BBY, ending the war. (clone-wars; [Balmorra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balmorra))
- The Galactic Empire used the factory sites after the war ended, and an Imperial facility there held clone troopers prisoner, siphoning them off to another facility. (clone-wars; low confidence; [Balmorra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balmorra))
**Legends**
- The planet seceded from the Galactic Republic in 22 BBY and was given a fleet of Lucrehulk-class battleships to defend it. (clone-wars; [Balmorra/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balmorra/Legends))
- As the Great Galactic War continued and the Sith Empire's control continued to spread, the strained Republic began removing forces from Balmorra for redeployment to more important conflicts. ([Balmorra/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balmorra/Legends))
- The Republic base designated 11A had been established on Balmorra and was still under Republic control until the Treaty was signed. ([Balmorra/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balmorra/Legends))
