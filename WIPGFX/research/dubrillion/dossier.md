## Political control
**Legends**
- With the onset of the Clone Wars, Dubrillion was located within Separatist space, though its resident sector of space was contested between both the CIS and the Galactic Republic. (clone-wars; [Dubrillion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dubrillion/Legends))
- Following the deaths of the Separatist Council members on Mustafar, and the end of the war, the planet fell to the rule of the Galactic Empire. (clone-wars; [Dubrillion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dubrillion/Legends))
- Dubrillion would duly become a world of the Galactic Empire after the Clone Wars; when that government fell, entrepreneur Lando Calrissian would take-over the planet and serve as its administrator. ([Dubrillion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dubrillion/Legends))
