## Battles and events
**Canon**
- An attack on Hanna City occurred early in the Clone Wars. (clone-wars; [Chandrila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chandrila))
- The planet's capital city was Hanna City which hosted the Chandrila Senate house; the site of the New Republic's Galactic Senate in the months following the Battle of Endor. ([Chandrila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chandrila))
- Following the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY, Chandrila became the capital of the restored Galactic Republic, the New Republic, the successor government to the Rebel Alliance. ([Chandrila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chandrila))
**Legends**
- Chandrila's era of peace ended during the Clone Wars when the Confederacy carried out an attack on Hanna City using a damotite bioweapon. (clone-wars; [Chandrila/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chandrila/Legends))
- The attack was orchestrated by Separatist General Lok Durd, while the weapon itself was created by the Human female scientist Bant'ena Fhernan. (clone-wars; [Chandrila/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chandrila/Legends))
- In the aftermath of the attack, Palpatine&mdash;now the Supreme Chancellor of the Republic&mdash;returned to Chandrila in an attempt to raise the morale of the people of the Core Worlds. (clone-wars; [Chandrila/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chandrila/Legends))
