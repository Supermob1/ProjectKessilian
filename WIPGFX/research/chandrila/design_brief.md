## Stated geographical relationships
- The Silver Sea was an expansive sea on the Core Worlds planet Chandrila. Located near the Chandrilan capital of Hanna City, multiple buildings in and around the capital had a view of the sea. [C] ([Silver Sea])
- The settlement Junari Point was located a few klicks outside Hanna City and hosted a bar. [C] ([Chandrila])
- Lake Sah'ot was also near Hanna City and was connected to the Silver Sea by the Jhanjaari River. [L] ([Chandrila/Legends])
- Chandrila's capital was Hanna City, located between the Silver Sea and Lake Sah'ot. [L] ([Chandrila/Legends])
