**Canon**

**Legends**
- The planet joined the Galactic Republic around 7000 BBY, but by the time of the Great Sith War, it had not integrated itself into the Republic as thoroughly as other planets. ([Trandosha/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandosha/Legends))

## Settlements

- Forak was the capital city of the planet of Trandosha. In downtown Forak, there was an Imperial repair center located on Banir Avenue. ([Forak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forak))
- Hsskhor was the capital city of the planet Trandosha. At the end of the New Sith Wars, it was the site of the Battle of Hsskhor, after the battle the city was razed to the ground. ([Hsskhor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hsskhor/Legends))
