## Stated geographical relationships
- Salis D'aar was the capital city of Bakura, It was located between two rivers on the planet, one being the West River, the other, the East River. It was founded by the leader of the first Human colonists to Bakura, Deredith Arden. [L] ([Salis D'aar/Legends])
- The Bakuran National Symphony was the most prestigious and elite musical group on Bakura. As it was the 'national' symphony, it was presumably located in the Bakuran capital of Salis D'aar. [L] ([Bakuran National Symphony])
- Bakura's main spaceport was located just south of Salis D'aar and on D'aarmont, and an Imperial garrison base identical to those found on many worlds was located a kilometer south of the spaceport. [L] ([Bakura/Legends])
