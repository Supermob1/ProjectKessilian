## Stated geographical relationships
- Kantiema was a river-port village on the planet Brentaal IV. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, the Galactic Empire maintained a presence in the village. [C] ([Kantiema])
- Cormond was the capital city of Brentaal IV. It lay along the salt flats between the Suporro Sea and the Gravaal Mountains. [L] ([Cormond])
- Oradin was a city on Brentaal IV, located to the west of the city of Vuultin. Oradin contained a large spaceport, Imperial-class. [L] ([Oradin])
- Favis Resort was an artificial island anchored in the mild southern polar sea of Brentaal IV. It was located near a variety of climates, so that visitors could climb nearby glaciers or sail boats. [L] ([Favis Resort])
- Votrad Stadium was located in the city of Votrad on Brentaal IV. It played host to a performance of The Kallea Cycle during the Galactic Civil War. [L] ([Votrad Stadium])
