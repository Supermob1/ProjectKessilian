## Visual appearance
**Canon**
- Batuu was once host to massive trees, though the giants were destroyed during a cataclysm that devastated the planet's surface. ([Batuu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Batuu))

- A smaller species of dark green conifers called Batuu trees grew in stony soil or clung to rocky surfaces across the planet. ([Batuu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Batuu))
