## Settlements
**Canon**
- A village located on the planet Shili was inhabited by members of the Togruta species, including a young Ahsoka Tano and her mother, Pav-ti Tano. It was located at the edge of a forested wilderness. ([Ahsoka Tano's village](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahsoka_Tano's_village))
- The native Togruta displayed distinctive skin patterns for camouflaging in the grasses. ([Shili](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shili))
**Legends**
- Corvala was the capital city of the planet Shili. It was a trade-focused city and was renowned for its masters of holistic medicine. ([Corvala](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corvala))
