STYLE BAR (user's pick): Felucia. Not in review.csv (it has no landmark meshes, only the hod_fel_city town set,
fel_city() in military_buildings.py, and the fel_* flora). What it does right: one strong planet-specific silhouette
(giant mushroom stalk with stacked hut rings), its own bright atlas (violet cap, orange pods, all well above 100 RGB),
3+ height levels, props on every level. Use it as the bar when rebuilding the town samples flagged TOWN.
In game (user screenshot): what sells Felucia is the flora carpet, not single buildings. Purple caps, red spiky
plants and gold bulbs cover the whole disc at several sizes, in one palette, with clouds layered over it. Lessons:
- Every planet needs a dense planet-specific prop/flora layer; most landmarks elsewhere sit alone on bare ground.
- Landmarks must be taller than the flora: Felucia's own sites (Commerce Guild HQ, Kway Teow, Nigkoe, Ancient Abyss)
  can't be picked out at that zoom. Give them a silhouette that rises above the mushroom caps.
- Dark spiky plants around Ban Nam and Ancient Abyss read near-black: same 100 RGB rule applies to flora. Raxus Prime, Kashyyyk, Serenno, Trandosha, Kalee, Chandrila and Ithor are the strongest sets.

PROBLEMS SEEN ACROSS MANY PLANETS (fixing these in shared code clears most P2s):
1. Black faces. Dark rock bases, slab skirts and DARK/SOOT swatches render black even in the review renders, so they
   will be black in game. Worst planets: Oba Diah, Dorin, Nar Shaddaa, Umbara, Yavin IV, Iego, Corellia (Bottoms), Shola.
   Raise the dark B_* swatches above 100 RGB in each planet atlas.
2. Town samples: at least 85 of 128 town samples have one or two very tall needle towers/poles that dwarf the town,
   and many are a sparse scatter of thin slivers with no ground. That is a town_cell issue, not a per-planet one.
   Cap tower height relative to the block, add more and wider blocks.
3. Floating parts: pads on poles, flags, discs, rails and boxes hover with no link to the base.
4. -y side blocked: low walls, slabs or hangars in front hide the main building (rule: tall parts at +y).
5. Reused kits: the "drums with ring pads" spaceport/dock and the "drum arena with floodlights" repeat on many planets
   (Commenor, Karfeddion, Nyriaan, Rattatak, Foerost, Loronar, Rendilli, Skako...). Rule: custom per planet.
6. Shipyards on the ground: many *_yards / *_works with ships sit on ground slabs. Rule: naval = orbital.
   Confirm their PLACEMENT puts them beside the disc; the ones built as ground frames need to become stations.
7. Halo rings and flat discs still appear (Ilum Tonvarr, Tibrin city, Wasskah lodge pad, Alderaan sanctuary).
8. Lone boxes under ~40 KB: almost every mesh under 40 KB is a P1 (one block, an obelisk, a plain pyramid).

WORST PLANET SETS (most P1s or key canon sites failing):
- Coruscant: Jedi Temple, Senate and most districts are small plain boxes. Most important planet, weakest set.
- Yavin IV: Great Temple is a 17 KB block; all three meshes black or plain.
- Kadavo: apart from the facility, everything is 16 to 21 KB boxes.
- Korriban: tombs are near-identical red cubes; obelisks are 10 KB.

FIX SHORTHANDS (used in the "Fix:" lines under each P1/P2 mesh):
  DARK     = raise the dark B_* swatches (DARK, SOOT, rock/skirt colours) in this planet's atlas above ~100 RGB,
             then `python -B "$S/military_buildings.py" <planet>_atlas`. Shape can stay.
  TOWN     = town_cell fix: cap the tallest tower at ~2x the average block height, double the block count and
             widen blocks, put them on a shared ground slab with a street grid, delete bare poles and floating bits.
             Text after TOWN is the planet's flavour.
  ORBIT    = naval rule: rebuild as an orbital station and move its PLACEMENT beside the disc.
  PORT-KIT = stop using the shared "drums with ring pads" port: give this planet its own terminal shape,
             pads on the ground, parked shuttle()/gunship(), cargo and a control tower.
  "Seat X" = X floats; put it on the ground or on legs()/supports.
  "Move X to +y" = X is on the camera side and hides the model; move it behind or delete it.
